## Main Features

- HUMAN — On-device human detection
- THERMAL — Real-time pseudothermal visualization
- MESH — LiDAR spatial mapping on compatible devices
- CAPTURE — Save scanner captures to your Photos library

## Device Compatibility

Some features depend on the hardware available on your iPhone.

MESH mode requires a compatible LiDAR-equipped device.

THERMAL mode is a pseudothermal visual effect generated from the standard iPhone camera image. It does not measure temperature and does not provide true infrared or thermal imaging.

HUMAN mode detects human figures in the camera image. It does not perform facial recognition or identify individuals.

## Privacy

Camera processing and human detection are performed on the device. ALIEN VISION does not use HUMAN mode to identify individuals.
