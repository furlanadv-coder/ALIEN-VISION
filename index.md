# ALIEN VISION

## Support

ALIEN VISION is a futuristic visual scanning experience for iPhone featuring HUMAN detection, pseudothermal visualization and LiDAR spatial mapping on compatible devices.

### Features

- HUMAN — On-device human detection
- THERMAL — Real-time pseudothermal visualization
- MESH — ARKit and LiDAR spatial mapping on compatible devices
- CAPTURE — Save scanner captures to your Photos library

### Important Information

THERMAL is a pseudothermal visual effect generated from the standard iPhone camera image. It does not measure temperature or provide true infrared or thermal imaging.

HUMAN detects human figures visible in the camera image. It does not perform facial recognition, biometric identification or determine a person's identity.

MESH requires compatible device hardware.

ALIEN VISION is intended for visual exploration and entertainment and is not a medical, diagnostic, safety, security or professional surveillance tool.
